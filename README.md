# JAPI - Human-Centered Operational Intelligence

JAPI is a Caribbean-built platform and methodology for turning fragmented information into traceable, human-controlled action.

It organizes objectives, context, evidence, workflows, recommendations, approvals, verification and review into one governed process. JAPI is designed to strengthen human judgment, not replace it.

## Future Caribbean Buildathon outcome

During the Buildathon, JAPI advanced from a documented methodology into a working private prototype with:

- role-specific user journeys;
- evidence provenance and confidence labeling;
- Human Pause checkpoints for consequential actions;
- access-control and recovery testing;
- browser-based persona validation;
- demonstration workflows spanning learning, business, tourism, transport, vehicle/fleet and public-service contexts.

## Current status

**Self-assessed TRL: 5/10**

JAPI has a working private prototype and founder-led validation evidence. It has not reached TRL 6 because no approved design-partner field pilot with measured real-world outcomes has been verified.

The current demonstrations use synthetic or non-live data. JAPI is not represented as a deployed government system, autonomous decision-maker, certified professional service or production-ready platform.

## Evidence boundaries

JAPI currently claims:

- a working private MVP;
- documented architecture and governance;
- reproducible build and internal test evidence;
- 17 role-specific journeys evaluated in controlled testing;
- public pilot-design and readiness materials.

JAPI does **not** claim:

- paying customers or recurring revenue;
- verified field deployment or measured field outcomes;
- confirmed institutional partners;
- external certification or independent security approval;
- autonomous authority over consequential decisions.
