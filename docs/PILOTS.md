# Public Pilot Portfolio

JAPI's public Data Room contains evaluation briefs for three bounded pilot concepts. Each is a proposal for discovery and evaluation, not a confirmed partnership or deployment.

## Mechanics Learning

A supervised diagnostic-learning workflow for technical learners. It would connect symptoms, safety checks, measurements, fault isolation, supervisor approval and post-repair verification.

Current state: desk research and pilot design. No host, cohort, curriculum approval or measured learning result is claimed.

## Food Export Planning

An export-readiness workspace for producers and processors covering capacity, standards, testing, packaging, logistics, documentation, buyer evidence and unit economics.

Current state: desk research and workflow design. No producer cohort, buyer, shipment, approval or sale is claimed.

## Blue Coastal Nexus Hub

A bounded coastal innovation and resilience concept using approved data, asset records and human observations to improve inspection, maintenance evidence, learning and scenario planning.

Current state: framework-level concept. No site, engineering approval, host commitment, construction activity or field outcome is claimed.

## Selection principle

JAPI should begin with the narrowest pilot that can be supervised safely, measured clearly and delivered within realistic founder and partner capacity.
