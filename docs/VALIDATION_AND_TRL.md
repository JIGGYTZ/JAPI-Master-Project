# Validation and TRL

## Self-assessed TRL 5

TRL 5 is supported by a working private prototype, versioned build evidence, role-specific journeys and founder-led validation in a controlled environment.

TRL 6 is not claimed because JAPI has no approved design-partner field pilot with measured real-world outcomes.

## Controlled test evidence

A Windows browser-persona audit for a recent candidate reported:

- 17 of 17 accessible persona accounts passed;
- 17 of 17 golden journeys passed;
- 17 of 17 recovery paths passed;
- 100 rendered routes and 100 available modules were inspected;
- 12 unavailable upgrades were truthfully disabled;
- zero accessibility findings were reported by that audit;
- one privileged account was correctly blocked.

These results describe the tested candidate and test environment. They are internal engineering evidence, not independent certification, production approval or proof of market demand.

## Open gates

- independent security, privacy and accessibility review;
- production identity and provider validation;
- signed installer/device evidence where applicable;
- authorized live-data agreements;
- legal and domain-professional review;
- verified customer discovery and willingness to pay;
- a supervised field pilot with baseline and outcome measurement.
